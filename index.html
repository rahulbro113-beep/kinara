Sakhi One Stop Centres

Most districts have one. In one place you can get medical care, help with the police, free legal aid, counselling, and a safe place to stay for up to 5 days. You can bring your daughters, and your sons up to age 12.

Call 181 to find the one nearest you.

What happens when you call

  1. Someone picks up. You can start with a simple question, like "What can you help me with?"
  2. They ask what is happening and listen. You decide how much to share.
  3. They explain your options: counselling, legal help, a safe place to stay, or the police.
  4. Before you give your name or address, you can ask what they will do with it.

If calling from home is not safe, use a friend's phone, or ask someone you trust to call 181 for you.

What the law says

The Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 protects women from abuse by the people they live with as family.

  • It is not only hitting. Insults, threats, sexual abuse, and keeping money or basic needs from you also count as domestic violence.
  • It protects daughters, sisters, mothers and widows too, not only wives.
  • A magistrate can order the abuser to stop, and can stop you from being thrown out of the home you share.
  • You can also ask for money for your expenses, custody of your children, and compensation.
  • Each district has Protection Officers, often women, whose job is to help you apply.
  • Breaking a protection order is a crime.

This is general information, not legal advice. A free legal aid lawyer on 15100 can explain what applies to you.

If someone you love is going through this

  • Believe her. Never ask what she did to cause it.
  • Ask what she wants, and respect it, even if you would choose differently.
  • Keep what she tells you private.
  • You can call 181 on her behalf.
  • Help her save these numbers somewhere only she can find them.

Masala chai for two

  • 1 cup water and 1 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons tea leaves
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • 2 cardamom pods and a small piece of ginger, crushed
  1. Boil the water with the ginger and cardamom for 2 minutes.
  2. Add the tea leaves and simmer for 1 minute.
  3. Add the milk and sugar and let it rise to a boil twice.
  4. Strain into cups and serve hot.
